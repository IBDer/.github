# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

IBDer welcomes patients, caregivers, clinicians, researchers, designers, developers, and anyone who wants to improve life with IBD. Different forms of knowledge belong here, but no participant is required to disclose a diagnosis or personal medical history.

IBDer 欢迎患者、照护者、临床与科研人员、设计师、开发者，以及所有希望改善 IBD 生活体验的人。我们尊重不同形式的知识，但任何参与者都不需要公开自己的诊断或个人病史。

## Expected behavior / 我们期待的行为

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Respect privacy and ask before sharing another person's story or data.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Make space for patient experience without presenting one experience as universal medical evidence.
- Avoid stigmatizing language about bodies, symptoms, disability, food, or treatment choices.

- 保持尊重、耐心和具体。
- 可以批评观点和证据，但不要攻击个人。
- 尊重隐私；分享他人的经历或数据前必须获得许可。
- 主动说明不确定性，并公开更正错误。
- 尊重患者经验，但不要把个体经验当作普遍医学证据。
- 避免对身体、症状、残障、饮食或治疗选择使用污名化语言。

## Unacceptable behavior / 不可接受的行为

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate exposure of health information, impersonation, spam, and knowingly dangerous medical claims are not acceptable.

我们不接受骚扰、威胁、歧视、性化关注、人肉搜索、故意泄露健康信息、冒充他人、垃圾信息，以及明知具有危险性的医疗主张。

## Enforcement / 执行

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban accounts when needed to protect the community. Reports should be made privately to an organization owner through GitHub and should include only the information necessary to assess the incident.

为保护社区，维护者可以编辑或删除内容、限制参与或封禁账号。相关问题应通过 GitHub 私下报告给组织所有者，并只提供评估事件所需的信息。
