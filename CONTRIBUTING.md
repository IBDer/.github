# Contributing to IBDer / 参与 IBDer

Thank you for helping build open tools and trustworthy research for people living with IBD.

感谢你帮助我们为 IBD 患者建设开放工具与可信研究。

## Before opening a contribution / 提交之前

1. Search existing issues and discussions to avoid duplicate work.
2. For substantial changes, open an issue describing the problem, intended users, evidence or design basis, and safety implications.
3. Do not include identifiable health information, private messages, medical records, credentials, or other secrets.
4. Clearly distinguish personal experience, expert opinion, observational evidence, and clinical evidence.

1. 请先搜索已有 Issue 和 Discussion，避免重复工作。
2. 较大的改动请先创建 Issue，说明问题、目标用户、证据或设计依据，以及可能的安全影响。
3. 不要提交可识别的健康信息、私人消息、医疗记录、凭证或其他秘密。
4. 请明确区分个人经历、专家意见、观察性证据和临床证据。

## Ways to contribute / 参与方式

- Research questions, literature sources, and evidence reviews
- Documentation and Chinese-English translation
- Accessibility, product design, and usability feedback
- Code, tests, specifications, and integrations
- Privacy, security, and medical-safety reviews

- 研究问题、文献来源和证据综述
- 文档与中英文翻译
- 可访问性、产品设计和易用性反馈
- 代码、测试、规范和集成
- 隐私、安全与医疗风险审查

## Research contributions / 调研贡献

Research contributions must follow [RESEARCH_POLICY.md](RESEARCH_POLICY.md). Every published research document must state its status, last search date, sources, evidence limitations, and reviewer state.

调研内容必须遵循 [RESEARCH_POLICY.md](RESEARCH_POLICY.md)。每份公开调研都必须注明状态、最后检索日期、来源、证据局限和审阅情况。

## Product and code contributions / 产品与代码贡献

- Explain the user problem before proposing a solution.
- Keep medical and safety boundaries visible in user-facing flows.
- Prefer small, reviewable changes with tests where practical.
- Follow the repository-specific development and licensing instructions.
- New integrations should document the data exchanged, consent model, and failure behavior.

- 在提出方案前先说明用户问题。
- 在面向用户的流程中清楚呈现医疗与安全边界。
- 优先提交小而易于审阅的改动，并在适用时提供测试。
- 遵循各仓库自己的开发与许可证说明。
- 新增集成必须说明交换的数据、授权方式和失败行为。

## Review / 审阅

Maintainers may request changes for evidence quality, privacy, safety, accessibility, scope, or maintainability. A contribution may be technically correct and still require additional review before publication.

维护者可能因证据质量、隐私、安全、可访问性、范围或可维护性要求修改。即使技术实现正确，也可能需要额外审阅后才能公开。
