# Governance / 治理

IBDer is founder-led during its early stage. This keeps responsibility clear while the community, scope, and review processes are still forming.

IBDer 在早期阶段由创始人负责最终决策，以便在社区、范围和审阅流程尚未成熟时保持责任清晰。

## Principles / 原则

- Patient welfare, privacy, and clarity take priority over growth or feature count.
- Decisions affecting public scope, safety boundaries, licensing, or interoperability are documented in public issues or decision records.
- Maintainer authority is based on sustained contribution, sound judgment, and respectful collaboration.
- Conflicts of interest should be disclosed where they could affect a decision or publication.

- 患者福祉、隐私和信息清晰度优先于增长或功能数量。
- 影响公开范围、安全边界、许可证或互操作性的决策，应记录在公开 Issue 或决策记录中。
- 维护者权限基于持续贡献、可靠判断和友善协作。
- 可能影响决策或发布内容的利益冲突应主动披露。

## Decision process / 决策流程

1. Discuss significant proposals in public whenever privacy and security allow.
2. Record the problem, options considered, evidence, risks, and final decision.
3. The founder or designated maintainer makes the final call when consensus is not reached.
4. Revisit decisions when new evidence, safety concerns, or community needs emerge.

1. 在隐私和安全允许的情况下，重要提案应公开讨论。
2. 记录问题、备选方案、依据、风险和最终决定。
3. 无法形成共识时，由创始人或指定维护者作出最终决定。
4. 出现新证据、安全问题或社区需求时，应重新审视既有决定。

The governance model should be reviewed after the first stable public release or when multiple active maintainers are ready to share responsibility.

在首个稳定公开版本发布后，或已有多名活跃维护者可以共同承担责任时，应重新审视治理模式。
