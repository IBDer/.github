# Research Publication Policy / 调研发布规范

IBDer publishes research as a transparent work product, not as medical advice. The goal is to make claims traceable, limitations visible, and updates manageable.

IBDer 将调研视为可追溯、可审阅的开放成果，而不是医疗建议。我们的目标是让观点有出处、局限可见、内容可持续更新。

## Required metadata / 必填元数据

Every research document must include:

- **Title / 标题**
- **Status / 状态**: `Draft`, `Reviewed`, or `Superseded`
- **Last searched / 最后检索日期**: ISO date, for example `2026-10-08`
- **Authors and reviewers / 作者与审阅者**
- **Question and scope / 问题与范围**
- **Sources / 来源**: stable identifiers or links where possible
- **Evidence summary / 证据摘要**
- **Limitations and uncertainty / 局限与不确定性**
- **Conflicts of interest / 利益冲突**
- **Supersedes or superseded by / 版本关系**, when applicable

## Publication states / 发布状态

- `Draft`: open for discussion; not independently reviewed.
- `Reviewed`: reviewed for source traceability, scope, and clear presentation of uncertainty. This does not mean clinical endorsement.
- `Superseded`: retained for history but replaced by a newer document.

- `Draft`：开放讨论，尚未完成独立审阅。
- `Reviewed`：已检查来源可追溯性、范围和不确定性表达，但不代表临床认可。
- `Superseded`：保留历史记录，但已由更新版本替代。

## Evidence labels / 证据标签

Use the narrowest label that accurately describes the source:

- `Guideline or consensus / 指南或共识`
- `Systematic review / 系统综述`
- `Randomized study / 随机研究`
- `Observational study / 观察性研究`
- `Preclinical or mechanistic / 临床前或机制研究`
- `Expert opinion / 专家意见`
- `Patient experience / 患者经验`
- `Unknown or unverified / 未知或未核实`

Do not combine these labels into a single numerical score until a documented, reviewed grading method exists.

在形成经过记录和审阅的评级方法之前，不应将这些标签合并为单一数字评分。

## Claims we do not publish / 不发布的结论

IBDer does not publish content that:

- diagnoses a person or recommends an individualized treatment;
- presents correlation as proof that a food, behavior, or exposure caused a symptom or flare;
- claims clinical efficacy without appropriately scoped evidence;
- calculates or interprets a disease activity score without authorized, validated use;
- exposes identifiable patient information;
- reproduces unlicensed clinical scales, images, or translations.

IBDer 不发布以下内容：

- 对个人作出诊断或提供个体化治疗建议；
- 将相关性表述为某种食物、行为或暴露导致症状或复发的因果证据；
- 在证据不足或范围不当时宣称临床疗效；
- 在未经授权和验证的情况下计算或解释疾病活动评分；
- 暴露可识别的患者信息；
- 复制未获许可的临床量表、图片或翻译。

## Corrections / 更正

Material corrections must be recorded in version history. A superseded document remains accessible with a prominent link to its replacement. Safety-critical errors should be reported privately according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

重要更正必须记录在版本历史中。被替代的文档应继续保留，并醒目标注新版本链接。涉及安全的严重错误应按照 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 私下报告。
