# IBDer Roadmap / IBDer 路线图

This roadmap communicates direction, not fixed release dates. Work advances when its evidence, safety, privacy, licensing, and maintenance requirements are understood.

本路线图用于说明方向，而不是承诺固定发布日期。只有在证据、安全、隐私、许可证和维护要求得到充分理解后，工作才会进入下一阶段。

## 0. Foundation / 基础建设

**Status: in progress / 状态：进行中**

- Establish the organization profile, governance, contribution paths, and safety boundaries.
- Publish bilingual research templates and a machine-readable catalog.
- Select the first research questions with clear patient relevance and manageable scope.

- 建立组织主页、治理方式、贡献路径和安全边界。
- 发布双语调研模板和机器可读目录。
- 选择首批与患者密切相关且范围可控的研究问题。

## 1. Open research / 开放调研

- Publish topic reviews with traceable sources, search dates, evidence types, limitations, and review history.
- Test the `Draft → Reviewed → Superseded` lifecycle on real work.
- Develop a sustainable correction and update process.

- 发布包含可追溯来源、检索日期、证据类型、局限和审阅记录的主题调研。
- 在真实内容中验证 `Draft → Reviewed → Superseded` 生命周期。
- 建立可持续的更正与更新流程。

## 2. Open specifications / 开放规范

- Define shared terminology and minimal metadata based on needs found during research.
- Document evidence labels, contributor roles, project status, versioning, and provenance.
- Keep formats interoperable with open-source and open-knowledge ecosystems where practical.

- 根据调研中发现的真实需求定义公共术语和最小元数据。
- 记录证据标签、贡献者角色、项目状态、版本和来源关系。
- 在可行范围内保持与开源和开放知识生态的互操作性。

## 3. Patient tools / 患者工具

- Validate specific patient problems before building features.
- Design privacy, informed consent, accessibility, and medical-safety behavior before collecting data.
- Build small, testable tools that support understanding and daily management without replacing professional care.

- 在开发功能前验证具体患者问题。
- 在收集数据前完成隐私、知情授权、可访问性和医疗安全设计。
- 建设小而可验证的工具，帮助患者理解和管理日常生活，但不替代专业医疗服务。

## 4. Integrations / 生态集成

- Publish stable project and research metadata for platforms such as OpenKHub.
- Add integrations only when data scope, consent, provenance, failure behavior, and maintenance ownership are clear.
- Prefer public knowledge and project metadata before any sensitive health-data exchange.

- 为 OpenKHub 等平台提供稳定的项目与调研元数据。
- 只有在数据范围、授权、来源、失败行为和维护责任明确后才增加集成。
- 优先交换公开知识与项目元数据，不优先处理敏感健康数据。

## Explicitly out of scope for the initial stages / 初期明确不做

- Diagnosis, treatment recommendations, emergency services, or remote consultation
- Patient forums or peer-to-peer medical advice
- Disease activity scoring or claims about food causality and clinical efficacy
- Identifiable patient records or health-record exchange
- Unlicensed clinical scales, images, or translations

- 诊断、治疗建议、紧急服务或远程问诊
- 患者论坛或患者之间的医疗建议
- 疾病活动评分，或关于食物因果关系与临床疗效的判断
- 可识别的患者记录或健康档案交换
- 未经授权的临床量表、图片或翻译

## How priorities change / 如何调整优先级

Material roadmap changes should be discussed publicly and record the user need, supporting evidence, expected benefit, risks, dependencies, and maintenance owner. Privacy or security details may be handled privately when disclosure would increase harm.

重要的路线图调整应公开讨论，并记录用户需求、支持证据、预期收益、风险、依赖和维护责任人。如果公开细节会增加伤害，隐私或安全信息可以私下处理。
