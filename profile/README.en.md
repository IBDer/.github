# IBDer

**Open tools and shared research for living with IBD.**

## Why IBDer?

Life with IBD extends far beyond the clinic. Symptom and medication records, food and daily routines, test results, appointment preparation, information quality, and long-term uncertainty all create work for patients and caregivers.

The relevant knowledge is fragmented across papers, guidelines, products, community experience, and personal records. Sources are hard to trace, evidence boundaries are often unclear, tools do not connect, and patients rarely get to define the problem.

IBDer is building an open foundation to:

- make important questions public;
- clarify sources, evidence, and uncertainty;
- turn reusable methods into open specifications;
- build tools around validated needs and connect them to a wider open ecosystem.

We do not make medical decisions for patients. We help people find the basis for claims, understand the limits, and participate in building better solutions.

## From real questions to public work

| 01 · Frame the question | 02 · Research openly | 03 · Establish specifications | 04 · Build tools |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Patients, caregivers, and practitioners identify a focused problem. | Methods, sources, evidence types, limitations, and review history remain visible. | Reusable terminology, metadata, project states, and interoperability conventions emerge from real needs. | Small, testable products gradually connect with the wider open ecosystem. |

Research follows a **`Draft → Reviewed → Superseded`** lifecycle. `Reviewed` means the sourcing and presentation were reviewed; it does not mean clinical endorsement.

## Project map

We are deliberately establishing sound research methods and safety boundaries before creating a collection of empty repositories. See the [public roadmap](https://github.com/IBDer/.github/blob/main/ROADMAP.md).

## Our principles

| Patients are contributors | Evidence stays traceable |
| --- | --- |
| Patients can frame questions, define scope, and review experiences—not merely use the final tool. | We distinguish guidelines, studies, expert opinion, and patient experience instead of hiding them behind one score. |
| **Privacy and safety before features** | **Open by default, designed for continuation** |
| We do not publish identifiable patient data or trade privacy for growth. | Problems, methods, decisions, and improvements stay open so others can review, reuse, and continue the work. |

Patients and caregivers, clinicians and researchers, product managers, designers, developers, data practitioners, translators, and documentation contributors are all welcome. You never need to disclose a diagnosis or medical history to participate.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> IBDer does not provide diagnosis, individualized treatment advice, disease activity scoring, or emergency services. We do not determine whether a particular food caused an individual's symptoms. Our work is not a substitute for professional medical judgment. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
