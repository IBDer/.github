# IBDer

**Open tools and shared research for living with IBD.**  
**为 IBD 患者共建开放工具与可信研究。**

[中文](#中文) · [English](#english)

## 中文

IBDer 是一个以患者为先的开源社区。我们希望把分散的炎症性肠病（IBD）信息、研究方法和数字工具，整理成任何人都能查看、质疑、改进和复用的公共成果。

### 我们正在建设

- **开放调研**：记录来源、检索日期、证据状态和适用范围的主题研究。
- **数据与规范**：为术语、元数据、证据等级和项目互操作性建立开放约定。
- **患者工具**：帮助患者记录、理解和管理日常生活，但不替代专业医疗服务。
- **生态连接**：让 IBDer 的项目可以与 OpenKHub 等开放知识与开源生态连接。

### 当前阶段

我们会先发布调研方法与主题研究，再逐步开放数据规范、应用和集成能力。项目仍处于早期阶段，欢迎患者、照护者、临床与科研人员、设计师和开发者参与讨论。具体顺序与边界见[公开路线图](../ROADMAP.md)。

### 安全边界

IBDer 不提供诊断、治疗建议或急救服务，也不声称能够判断食物因果关系、临床疗效或疾病严重程度。这里的内容不能替代医生的判断。遇到紧急情况，请立即联系当地急救服务或专业医疗人员。

我们不会收集或公开可识别的患者信息，也不会在没有明确授权的情况下使用临床量表、图片或翻译内容。

### 参与方式

- 提出研究问题、补充可靠来源或指出证据缺口
- 改进文档、翻译、可访问性和产品设计
- 参与开放规范、工具和集成开发
- 友善地分享患者视角，同时保护个人隐私

参与前请阅读 [贡献指南](../CONTRIBUTING.md)、[研究发布规范](../RESEARCH_POLICY.md)和[行为准则](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)。

## English

IBDer is a patient-first open-source community. We turn fragmented information, research methods, and digital tools for inflammatory bowel disease into public work that anyone can inspect, challenge, improve, and reuse.

### What we are building

- **Open research** with traceable sources, search dates, evidence status, and clearly stated limits.
- **Data and specifications** for terminology, metadata, evidence levels, and interoperability.
- **Patient tools** that support daily tracking and understanding without replacing professional care.
- **Ecosystem integrations** that help IBDer projects connect with open-source and open-knowledge platforms such as OpenKHub.

### Current stage

We will publish our research methods and topic reviews first, followed by open specifications, applications, and integrations. IBDer is at an early stage, and we welcome patients, caregivers, clinicians, researchers, designers, and developers. See the [public roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) for sequencing and boundaries.

### Safety boundaries

IBDer does not provide diagnosis, treatment advice, or emergency services. We do not claim to determine food causality, clinical efficacy, or disease severity. Our work is not a substitute for professional medical judgment. In an emergency, contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional.

We do not collect or publish identifiable patient information, and we do not reuse clinical scales, images, or translations without appropriate permission.

### Contribute

- Propose research questions, add reliable sources, or identify evidence gaps
- Improve documentation, translation, accessibility, and product design
- Build open specifications, tools, and integrations
- Share patient perspectives respectfully while protecting personal privacy

Before contributing, please read our [contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md), [research publication policy](../RESEARCH_POLICY.md), and [code of conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
